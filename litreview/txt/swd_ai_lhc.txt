Application 2—— SWD Consistency test
Phys. Rev. D 111, 096017
Idea: SWD + permutation test provides a 
model-independent way to quantify whether 
two high-dimensional distributions are 
statistically compatible.
Given two samples:
𝑋 ~ 𝑃, 𝑌 ∼ 𝑄 
1. Compute the observed distance: 𝑆𝑊𝐷 𝑋, 𝑌
2. Randomly permute sample labels many times.
3. Build the null distribution of SWD under 𝑃 = 𝑄.
4. Convert the observed SWD into a p-value.
Usage
• Check event generator performance 
• Test MC agreement with real data
